# Senate timeline, 114th-119th Congress (2015-2026)

Party control is at the start of each Congress. Rows marked *(data)* are visible in our Voteview
cache (117th-119th). Other items are general history. Items marked *verify* need a primary source.

## 114th Congress (2015-2016): Republican majority 54-46; Leader McConnell; President Obama

- 2015: the Senate takes up Trade Promotion Authority, and cloture fails before succeeding on a
  reworked vehicle.
- 2015-10: Bipartisan Budget Act of 2015, including a debt-limit suspension.
- 2016-02 to 2017-01: Garland Supreme Court nomination. No hearing; returned at sine die.
- 2016-09-28: **JASTA veto overridden**, 97-1. The only override of Obama.
- The 2017 CRA lookback window for late-Obama rules opens at the start of the 115th.

## 115th Congress (2017-2018): R 52-48 (51-49 after Jones, Jan 2018); McConnell; President Trump

- 2017-02-07: Rule XIX invoked against Sen. Warren.
- 2017: **16 CRA disapprovals enacted**.
- 2017-04-06: **nuclear option for SCOTUS cloture** (Gorsuch).
- 2017-07-28: ACA "skinny repeal" reconciliation fails 49-51.
- 2017-12: **TCJA** passes by reconciliation (P.L. 115-97).
- 2017-18: Judiciary Chair Grassley stops treating blue slips as a veto for circuit nominees.
- 2018-01-20 to 01-22: shutdown. 2018-02-09: brief lapse; Bipartisan Budget Act of 2018.
- 2018-06-20: ICA rescission package fails 48-50.
- 2018-10-06: Kavanaugh confirmed 50-48.
- 2018-12-22: the 35-day partial shutdown begins (ends 2019-01-25).

## 116th Congress (2019-2020): R 53-47; McConnell; President Trump

- 2019-03-14: border-wall NEA termination passes 59-41 and is vetoed. Override fails in the House.
- 2019-04-03: **nuclear option: 2-hour post-cloture** for district judges and sub-cabinet nominees.
- 2019: Yemen War Powers resolution (S.J.Res.7) and Saudi arms-sale disapprovals pass and are vetoed.
- 2020-01/02: first Trump impeachment trial. Witnesses rejected 49-51; acquitted 2020-02-05.
- 2020-01: USMCA under trade fast track.
- 2020-02/03: Iran War Powers (S.J.Res.68) passes 55-45 and is vetoed.
- 2020-03: CARES Act passes 96-0 under UC-structured cloture.
- 2020-10-26: Barrett confirmed 52-48.
- 2021-01-01: **FY2021 NDAA veto overridden** (81-13). Trump's only override.

## 117th Congress (2021-2022): 50-50 (Democrats control via VP Harris); Leader Schumer; President Biden

- 2021-01-06: electoral count objections fail (AZ 6-93, PA 7-92). *(data)*
- 2021-01-26: motion to table Paul's constitutional point of order on trying a former President,
  55-45. *(data)*
- 2021-02: **S.Res.27 power-sharing**: equal committees and a tied-committee discharge motion. 24
  nomination discharges through 2022-08. *(data)*
- 2021-02-04/05: FY2021 budget resolution vote-a-rama (`SCONRES5`), with many 50-50 ties. *(data)*
- 2021-02-13: Trump acquitted 57-43 in the second trial. *(data)*
- 2021-02-25: the parliamentarian advises that the $15 minimum wage is extraneous under the Byrd rule.
- 2021-03-06: **ARP** passes by reconciliation after a 34-roll-call vote-a-rama (`HR1319`). *(data)*
- 2021: 3 CRA disapprovals enacted.
- 2021-08-10: IIJA passes. FY2022 budget resolution vote-a-rama (S.Con.Res.14). *(data)*
- 2021-10 / 2021-12: debt limit raised. The Dec 2021 one-time fast track (S.610) is followed by a
  majority increase. *(verify P.L. numbers)*
- 2022-01-19: **nuclear option attempt fails**. The talking filibuster for voting rights (H.R.5746)
  is defeated when the chair is sustained 52-48. *(data)*
- 2022-04-07: Jackson confirmed 53-47.
- 2022-08-07: **IRA** passes 51-50 with the VP after a 37-roll-call vote-a-rama (`HR5376`), the
  busiest Senate day in our cache. *(data)*
- 2022-12: Electoral Count Reform Act enacted (in the FY2023 omnibus).

## 118th Congress (2023-2024): D 51-49; Schumer; President Biden

- 2023-02 to 2023-12: Tuberville military promotion holds.
- 2023-06: Fiscal Responsibility Act (debt limit) passes 63-36.
- 2023-06-21 to 2024-02-29: 5 failed overrides of Biden CRA vetoes. *(data)*
- 2024-01-16: 502B Israel human-rights resolution tabled 72-11. *(data)*
- 2024-03-23: final FY2024 appropriations package on shell `HR2882`, with a series of tabling
  votes. *(data)*
- 2024-04-17: **Mayorkas impeachment articles dismissed by points of order**. *(data)*
- 2024-07-11: Gaza pier War Powers resolution ruled non-privileged (point of order well taken
  48-46). *(data)*
- 2024-11-20: AECA Israel arms-sale disapprovals fail on discharge (17-19 yeas). *(data)*
- 2024-08: Sen. Menendez resigns after his conviction.

## 119th Congress (2025-2026): R 53-47; Leader Thune; President Trump

- 2025-01-24: Hegseth confirmed 50-50 with VP Vance. *(data)*
- 2025: **CRA wave**, with 24 Senate-passed disapproval resolutions through Oct 2026. *(data)*
- 2025-04-02 onward: NEA terminations of tariff emergencies. Some pass the Senate (Canada,
  2025-04-02, 51-48; Brazil, Canada and global, 2025-10-28/30). The House blocks them by rule.
  *(data)*
- 2025-05-21: **CRA California-waiver precedent** (`SJRES55` points of order sustained). The
  waivers are disapproved via H.J.Res.87-89. *(data)*
- 2025-06-30 / 07-01: **OBBBA** vote-a-rama. The chair is sustained on the Budget chair's baseline
  authority (53-47). The bill passes 51-50 with the VP (P.L. 119-21, signed 2025-07-04). *(data)*
- 2025-07-15: ICA rescissions (`HR4`) discharged 50-50 with the VP. Enacted later in July
  *(verify)*. *(data)*
- 2025-09-11: **nuclear option: en bloc nominations** by executive resolution (`SRES377`). En bloc
  resolutions follow: SRES412 (Oct), SRES532 (Dec), SRES690 (May 2026), SRES817 (Jul 2026). *(data)*
- 2025-10-01 to 11-12: **43-day shutdown**. H.R.5371 fails cloture 13 times, then passes cloture
  60-40 on Nov 9-10. *(data)*
- 2026-01-14: Venezuela War Powers resolution (`SJRES98`) ruled non-privileged on a 50-50 point
  of order. *(data)* VP tie-break *(verify)*.
- 2026-01-31 to 02-03: partial lapse. A DHS-only lapse begins 2026-02-14 *(end date verify)*.
- 2026-04-23: FY2026 budget resolution (`SCONRES33`) agreed 50-48. *(data)*
- 2026-06-23 / 09-24: House War Powers §5(c) concurrent resolutions: one agreed 50-48, one rejected
  49-50. *(data)*
- The 119th is still in session as of 2026-10-25. Re-query the cache for anything after the last
  load.
