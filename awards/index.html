What the award icons mean

Icons beside an official's name elsewhere on the site mean they hold that award now (the lists above are the awards, so they leave the icons off): they rank within the award's cutoff among those with the same office in the latest year loaded. X2, X3 and so on is the number of qualifying years in a row (qualifying cycles, for the federal money share, which is ranked per two-year FEC cycle). The icons are placeholders.

Highest share of sponsored House bills (HB, HJR) that passed the House Counts House bills (HB) and House joint resolutions (HJR) the member sponsored as the primary sponsor in sessions that have ended, out of those whose outcome is known (bills still pending are not counted), and shows the share the House passed. Appropriation bills are included; co-sponsored bills and resolutions (HR, HCR) are not. A year adds its regular and special sessions; at least 10 decided bills are needed, and at least one must have passed. Only current officials are in the data, so early years are not comparable with recent ones. Bills from the majority party pass far more often, and most members pass none, so there is no “fewest passed” list.
