All 638 sites that EPA or a state environmental program regulates in the area represented by State Representative, district 119 (Brad Banderman). The list is the same for every area: a site appears here because of where it is, not because of who owns it. A donor with no filing page has one line with an empty filing_page_url; a site with no matched donor has no line.

Limits: only EPA-regulated sites are listed (factories, fuel stations, utilities, repair shops, farms with permits), so offices, stores and most service businesses are missing. A site's presence says nothing about its conduct; being regulated is routine.
