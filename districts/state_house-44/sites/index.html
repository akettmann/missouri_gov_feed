All 553 sites that EPA or a state environmental program regulates in the area represented by State Representative, district 44 (John Martin). Limits: only EPA-regulated sites are listed (factories, fuel stations, utilities, repair shops, farms with permits), so offices, stores and most service businesses are missing. A site's presence says nothing about its conduct; being regulated is routine.
