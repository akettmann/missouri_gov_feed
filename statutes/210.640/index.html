Revised Statutes of Missouri · Chapter 210, Child Protection and Reformation · Title XII, PUBLIC HEALTH AND WELFARE

Section 210.640

The text below is word for word what the Revisor of Statutes publishes. We add nothing to it and leave nothing out. The Revisor lists 2 versions of this section.

In force · current version

In force since August 28, 2007.

210.640. Placements from nonparty states, procedure for. — No placement made into this state from a jurisdiction not party to the Interstate Compact on the Placement of Children shall be lawful unless the person or agency making the placement complies with and follows the procedures and requirements of that compact as though the state or jurisdiction from which the child is sent or brought were party thereto. This section shall not apply to any placement which would not be subject to the terms of the Interstate Compact on the Placement of Children if both this state and the other state or jurisdiction from which the child is sent or brought were parties thereto.

Revisor's notes

  1. (L. 1975 S.B. 162 § 7, A.L. 2007 S.B. 84)
  2. Contingent effective date, see § 210.650

In force (earlier version)

An earlier version that the Revisor still prints beside the current one; it took effect August 28, 1975.

*210.640. Placements from nonparty states, procedure for. — No placement made into this state from a jurisdiction not party to the Interstate Compact on the Placement of Children shall be lawful unless the person or agency making the placement complies with and follows the procedures and requirements of Article III of that compact as though the state or jurisdiction from which the child is sent or brought were party thereto. This section shall not apply to any placement which would not be subject to the terms of the Interstate Compact on the Placement of Children if both this state and the other state or jurisdiction from which the child is sent or brought were parties thereto.

Revisor's notes

  1. (L. 1975 S.B. 162 § 7)
  2. *This section was amended by S.B. 84, 2007. Due to the contingent effective date contained in section 210.650, both versions of this section are printed.
