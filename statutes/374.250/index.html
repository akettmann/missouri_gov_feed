Revised Statutes of Missouri · Chapter 374, Department of Commerce and Insurance · Title XXIV, BUSINESS AND FINANCIAL INSTITUTIONS

Section 374.250

The text below is word for word what the Revisor of Statutes publishes. We add nothing to it and leave nothing out.

In force · current version

In force since August 28, 2024.

374.250. Accounts of director. — 1. The director shall take proper vouchers for all payments made by the department and shall take receipts from the director of revenue for all moneys the department pays to the director of revenue.

2. At the close of each state fiscal year, the state auditor shall audit, adjust and settle all receipts and disbursements in the insurance dedicated fund and the insurance examiners' fund, and the results shall be reported as part of the annual audit of the state's financial statements.

Revisor's notes

  1. (RSMo 1939 § 5797, A. 1949 H.B. 2115, A.L. 2008 S.B. 788, A.L. 2024 H.B. 2111)
  2. Prior revisions: 1929 § 5687; 1919 § 6098; 1909 § 6892
