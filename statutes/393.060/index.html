In force · current version

In force since August 28, 1943.

393.060. Number of owners may be joined in one petition. — Any number of owners owning lands in the same county or judicial circuit may be joined in one petition, and the damages to each shall be separately assessed by the same commissioners or jury.

Revisor's notes

  1. (RSMo 1939 § 5375, A.L. 1943 p. 410 § 159)
  2. Prior revisions: 1929 § 4968; 1919 § 10178; 1909 § 3372
