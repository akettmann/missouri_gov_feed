Section 483.580

The text below is word for word what the Revisor of Statutes publishes. We add nothing to it and leave nothing out.

In force · current version

In force since July 1, 1997.

483.580. Fees, probate proceedings, guardianships. — 1. In all probate proceedings in the probate divisions of the different circuit courts in this state, there shall be charged against and collected from the estates or parties requiring the services of the probate division of the circuit court, court costs in such amounts as are determined pursuant to sections 488.010 to 488.020*.

2. Except as provided in section 483.550, the court costs provided in this section shall be charged and collected by the clerk of the probate division of the circuit court.

  2. Prior revisions: 1929 § 11782; 1919 § 10991; 1909 § 10695
  3. Effective 7-01-97
  4. *Words "section 514.015" appear in original rolls, which was changed to effectuate the court cost bill.
