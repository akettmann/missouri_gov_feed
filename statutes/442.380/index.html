In force since August 28, 1939.

442.380. Instruments to be recorded. — Every instrument in writing that conveys any real estate, or whereby any real estate may be affected, in law or equity, proved or acknowledged and certified in the manner herein prescribed, shall be recorded in the office of the recorder of the county in which such real estate is situated.

  2. Prior revisions: 1929 § 3039; 1919 § 2198; 1909 § 2809

  5. Idem sonans in names in instruments affecting real estate, rule as to admissibility, 490.450
  6. Index of recorded instruments to be kept, 59.470
  7. Instruments affecting real estate or copy read in evidence, when, 490.410, 490.420
  8. Judgment or decree quieting or passing title to be recorded, 511.320
  9. Recording of instruments in class one counties where recorder is required to maintain offices both at the county seat and another place in the county, 59.163
  10. (2004) Special tax bill and resulting lien authorized by section 88.812 are not subject to recording requirement of section or to "first in time, first in right" rule of perfecting a security interest. Golden Delta Enterprises v. City of Arnold, 151 S.W.3d 119 (Mo.App.E.D.).
  11.
