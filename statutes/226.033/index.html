Revised Statutes of Missouri · Chapter 226, Department of Transportation · Title XIV, ROADS AND WATERWAYS

Section 226.033

The text below is word for word what the Revisor of Statutes publishes. We add nothing to it and leave nothing out. The Revisor lists 2 versions of this section.

In force · current version

In force since August 28, 2010.

*226.033. Prohibited acts by certain commissioners. — Any commissioner appointed or reappointed after March 1, 2004, shall not:

(1) Host or manage a political fund-raiser or solicit funds for any candidate who is seeking a statewide or nationally elected office;

(2) Serve on the board or chair any political action committee, or political party committee.

Revisor's notes

  1. (L. 2003 H.B. 668, A.L. 2010 S.B. 844)
  2. *Revisor's Note: S.B. 844 in 2010 amended this section. S.B. 844 was declared unconstitutional (see annotation below). The other printed version of this section (the 2003 language from H.B. 668, effective 8/28/2003), reprinted in accordance with Section 3.066, is the current version.
  3. (2012) Senate Bill 844 provision declared unconstitutional as a violation of the original purpose requirement of Art. III, Sec. 21, Constitution of Missouri. Legends Bank v. State, 361 S.W.3d 383 (Mo. banc).
