Revised Statutes of Missouri · Chapter 32, Department of Revenue · Title IV, EXECUTIVE BRANCH

Section 32.315

The text below is word for word what the Revisor of Statutes publishes. We add nothing to it and leave nothing out.

In force · current version

In force since August 28, 2018.

32.315. Sales and use tax levies, department to issue annual report — contents. — 1. The department of revenue shall issue an annual report on or before January 1, 2019, and every January first thereafter, listing all sales and use * levies that are:

(1) Authorized pursuant to state law;

(2) Collected by the department of revenue; and

(3) Approved by voters at an election.

2. The report required under subsection 1 of this section shall indicate the provision of law authorizing such tax levy.

Revisor's notes

  1. (L. 2018 H.B. 1446 merged with S.B. 773)
  2. Revisor's Note: This section was amended by both H.B. 1446 and S.B. 773, 2018. In 2021, H.B. 1446 was declared unconstitutional in City of De Soto v. Parson, 625 S.W.3d 412 (Mo.banc). The amendments to this section in H.B. 1446 contained a one-word difference. As a result, the one word from H.B. 1446 has been removed and the amendments to this section in S.B. 773, 2018, remain in effect.
  3. *Word "tax" appears here in original rolls of H.B. 1446, 2018. In 2021, H.B. 1446 was declared unconstitutional in City of De Soto v. Parson, 625 S.W.3d 412 (Mo.banc).
