Revised Statutes of Missouri · Chapter 351, General and Business Corporations · Title XXIII, CORPORATIONS, ASSOCIATIONS AND PARTNERSHIPS

Section 351.020

The text below is word for word what the Revisor of Statutes publishes. We add nothing to it and leave nothing out.

In force · current version

In force since August 28, 1975.

351.020. What corporations may organize under this law. — Corporations for profit, except those which are required to be organized exclusively under other provisions of law, may be organized under this chapter for any lawful purposes.
