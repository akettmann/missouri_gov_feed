Revised Statutes of Missouri · Chapter 27, Attorney General · Title IV, EXECUTIVE BRANCH

Section 27.117

The text below is word for word what the Revisor of Statutes publishes. We add nothing to it and leave nothing out. The Revisor lists 2 versions of this section.

In force · current version

In force since August 28, 2026.

*27.117. Offense of disclosure of an intimate digital depiction and offense of sadistic online exploitation, attorney may assist in prosecution. — A prosecuting or circuit attorney may request assistance from the attorney general, or one of his or her assistants, to assist in the prosecution of a violation of section** 573.570 or 573.575, where the offense occurred in more than one jurisdiction of the state. The prosecuting or circuit attorney may request any resource or capability of the attorney general when prosecuting such cases.

Revisor's notes

  1. (L. 2026 H.B. 2273, et al.)
  2. *Revisor's note: This section was enacted by H.B. 2273, et al., H.B. 2637 & 3155, and S.B. 999, 2026. Due to a delayed effective date, both versions are printed.
  3. **Word "sections" appears in original rolls.

Not yet effective

Takes effect July 1, 2027.

27.117. Attorney general may assist in prosecution of certain offenses in chapters 565 and 573. — A prosecuting or circuit attorney may request assistance from the attorney general, or one of his or her assistants, to assist in the prosecution of a violation of section* 565.090, 565.091, 565.225, 565.227, 565.400, 565.405, 573.570, or 573.575, where the offense occurred in more than one jurisdiction of the state. The prosecuting or circuit attorney may request any resource or capability of the attorney general when prosecuting such cases.

Revisor's notes

  1. (L. 2026 H.B. 2273, et al. merged with H.B. 2637 & 3155 merged with S.B. 999)
  2. Effective 7-01-27, see § 565.019
  3. *Word "sections" appears in original rolls of H.B. 2273, et al., H.B. 2637 & 3155, and S.B. 999, 2026.
  4. Nonseverability clause, see § 1.1008
  5. Severability clause, see § 1.1005
