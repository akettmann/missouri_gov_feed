210.108. Dominic James Memorial Foster Care Reform Act, statutes involved. — Sections 37.700 to 37.730*, 168.133*, 191.748, 207.085, 210.109, 210.110, 210.111, 210.112, 210.113, 210.127, 210.145*, 210.147, 210.150, 210.152*, 210.153, 210.160, 210.183, 210.187, 210.188, 210.482, 210.487, 210.518, 210.535, 210.542, 210.565, 210.760, 210.762, 211.031, 211.032, 211.059, 211.319, and 537.046 shall be known and may be cited as the "Dominic James Memorial Foster Care Reform Act of 2004".

Revisor's notes

  1. (L. 2004 H.B. 1453 § 37.699)
  2. *Revisor's Note: Sections 37.710, 168.133, 210.145, and 210.152 are also included as part of the "Amy Hestir Student Protection Act" in section 160.250.
