Section 141.730

The text below is word for word what the Revisor of Statutes publishes. We add nothing to it and leave nothing out.

In force · current version

In force since August 28, 1943.

141.730. Officers of land trust — bond — oath. — 1. Such land trustees shall meet immediately after all three have been appointed and qualified and shall select a chairman, a vice chairman, a secretary, and an assistant secretary, who need not be a trustee.

2. Such trustees shall each furnish a surety bond in a penal sum not to exceed twenty-five thousand dollars to be approved by the collector, the premium on such bond to be advanced by the collector out of the county funds. Such bond must be issued by a surety company licensed to do business in the state of Missouri, which bond shall be deposited with the county clerk of such county, and shall be conditioned to guarantee the faithful performance of their duties hereunder, and shall be written to cover all the trustees.

3. Before entering upon the duties of his office, each trustee shall take and subscribe to the following oath:
