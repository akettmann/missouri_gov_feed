Only the Senate records recusals (the House source does not), so a dash means not recorded, not never. For a legislator who served in both chambers, the count covers only their Senate roll calls ("0 of 39 Senate"). Recused counts roll calls; the list shows one vote per bill (the latest), so a bill recused on at several roll calls appears once. Every bill with a recusal is listed, even past the most recent bills.
