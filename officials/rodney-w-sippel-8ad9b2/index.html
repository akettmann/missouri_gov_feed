Median days from the filing date to the termination date, over 145 federal criminal dockets in the Eastern District of Missouri assigned to this judge on the docket record (any filing year). 1 docket that ends before it was filed (a source error) is left out.

145 of 261 assigned criminal dockets measured (55.5%). Fewer than 80% are measured, and dockets still open are not counted, so this median may understate how long this judge's criminal cases take.

Civil and criminal dockets are counted apart because criminal cases tend to take longer than civil ones and the mix differs by judge. Miscellaneous dockets (warrants, subpoenas, complaints) and dockets of other or unclassified types are in neither. A docket counts only if CourtListener has both dates, so dockets still open are not counted. "Assigned" is the judge listed on the docket, not necessarily the judge who decided it.
