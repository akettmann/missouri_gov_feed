Pay per vote attended is gross pay from the state payroll (everything paid in the calendar year) divided by the votes in that calendar year where the official voted yea, nay or present. The total covers 1 of 2 years with votes (the rest have no pay counted). pay source↗ · How pay per vote is counted.

Amounts are what the state paid, not what was claimed, and each is the payee's total for the fiscal year, not one payment. The state's records carry legal names, so payees are matched to officials through the state payroll; "possible match" rows are matched on a looser fit (a nickname fit, or a surname shared with another legislator) and can be hidden above. No row for FY2018, FY2019, FY2020, FY2021, FY2022, FY2023, FY2024: the payee was not paid or could not be matched. Only people who hold an office on this site today are matched, so a former legislator who holds none is missing.
