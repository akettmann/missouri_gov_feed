What the State of Missouri paid this official as a legislator: a daily expense allowance (per diem) and in-state mileage, from the state's expenditure records, per state fiscal year (July to June).

No per diem or mileage payment is matched to this official. Payees are matched to current officials through the state payroll, and only people who hold an office on this site today are matched; a payee whose name fits more than one legislator is left out. This does not mean nothing was paid.

Amounts are what the state paid, not what was claimed, and each is the payee's total for the fiscal year, not one payment. The state's records carry legal names, so payees are matched to officials through the state payroll; "possible match" rows are matched on a looser fit (a nickname fit, or a surname shared with another legislator) and can be hidden above. Only people who hold an office on this site today are matched, so a former legislator who holds none is missing.
